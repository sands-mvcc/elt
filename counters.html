Ripple Counter Characteristics:

  • Only first flip-flop gets external clock
  • Each FF clocks the next one (cascade)
  • Propagation delay causes "ripple effect"
  • Slower operation due to cumulative delays
  • Simple design, fewer components

Synchronous Counter Characteristics:

  • All flip-flops share common clock
  • All outputs change simultaneously
  • No propagation delay between FFs
  • Faster operation, higher frequency
  • More complex design with logic gates
