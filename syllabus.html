Catalog Description

This course provides a practical approach to the study of digital and integrated devices. The course will include the study of logic gates, flip-flops, latches, counters, encoders/decoders, multiplexers, arithmetic circuits, oscillators, timers, analog-to-digital circuits, digital-to-analog circuits, solid-state memory, and operational amplifiers.

In addition, the course covers solid-state circuits and devices — including the study of diodes, transistors, FETs, thyristors, and optoelectronic devices. Examination of common circuits will include power supplies, amplifiers, solid-state switches, and regulators.

  • Number Systems and Binary Arithmetic
  • Logic Gates (AND, OR, NOT, NAND, NOR, XOR, XNOR)
  • Flip-Flops & Latches (SR, D-Type, JK)
  • Counters — Asynchronous and Synchronous
  • Encoders and Decoders
  • Multiplexers and Demultiplexers
  • A-to-D and D-to-A Conversion Circuits
  • Solid-State Memory
  • Diodes, Bipolar Transistors (BJT/UJT), FETs, and Thyristors
  • Optoelectronic Devices (LEDs, Photodiodes, Optocouplers)
  • Power Supplies and Voltage Regulators
  • Amplifiers and Operational Amplifiers (Op-Amps)
🎯

Course End Competencies

  • 1 Demonstrate the proper operation of logic gates, counters, encoders/decoders, and arithmetic circuits.
  • 2 Explain the operation of oscillators, timers, and operational amplifiers.
  • 3 Explain the operation of flip-flops, latches, A-to-D, D-to-A circuits, solid-state memory, and multiplexers.
  • 4 Demonstrate proper troubleshooting procedures and practices for digital circuit systems.
  • 5 Demonstrate the proper operation of semiconductor devices.
  • 6 Explain the operation of BJTs, UJT, FETs, and thyristors.
  • 7 Explain the operation of opto-electronic devices including LEDs, photodiodes, and optocouplers.
  • 8 Identify operating parameters and component ratings of common electronic devices.
  • 9 Demonstrate proper troubleshooting procedures and practices for analog/solid-state circuits.

Classroom Policies & Procedures

⏰
Attendance is expected at all class sessions. A 5-point penalty is deducted from participation points for missing class. A 1-point penalty is applied for arriving late. Emergency, family, and work-related situations are handled individually — please email the instructor as soon as possible.

Attendance, being on time, and turning in work on time represents 10% of your final grade. If you cannot attend class, email the instructor in advance.

A student who does not officially withdraw from a course may receive a grade of 'F' depending on course progress or attendance, which becomes part of the student's permanent record.

  • Turn in completed assignments when due. If you have a legitimate reason for not completing work on time (family emergency or severe illness), discuss this with the instructor prior to the due date.
  • There will be no test make-ups without prior arrangements.
  • The Final Exam will be online. You may use your notes and textbook.
🔁
Make-Up Policy Chapter review questions and quizzes may be completed after the due date, but with a penalty of 10% per day. If you miss class, hands-on labs must be completed before moving on to labs for the next module.
  • Attend all lab sessions and participate fully in each lab activity. Labs constitute 40% of the final grade.
  • No word-for-word collaboration on lab discussion questions and conclusions. Each student must write their own answers; otherwise only half the points are awarded. Team assignments will be designated as exceptions.
  • Lab reports must be submitted individually and reflect your own circuit observations, data, and analysis.

Lab Safety Notice Students with any condition that may impair their ability to concentrate must discuss this with the instructor before operating equipment or performing a laboratory procedure. Failure to notify the instructor may be a violation of the Code of Student Conduct.
