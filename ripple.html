How Ripple Counters Work
• Each flip-flop triggers the next when its output goes from 1→0
• The clock signal "ripples" through each stage with propagation delay
• Total delay = Number of flip-flops × Individual propagation delay
• Max frequency = 1 / (Total propagation delay)
• Watch the animation to see the ripple effect in action!
